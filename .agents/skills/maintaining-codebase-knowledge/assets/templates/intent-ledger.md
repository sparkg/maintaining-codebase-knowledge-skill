# Development intent ledger

Last verified: revision and date when material

| Intent | Source snapshot | Target capability or flow | Readiness | Missing input or evidence | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Feature, bug, optimization, migration, refactor, test gap, or infrastructure intent | Path, canonical ID/link, version, and retrieval time when material | Canonical document link or unrouted | ready, needs-deepen, needs-decision, or blocked | Task-specific requirement, decision, evidence, or environment blocker | Proceed, Deepen, obtain decision, acquire evidence, or resolve blocker |

Do not copy implementation paths, invariants, test lists, or capability-local gaps into this ledger. Link their canonical owner.

Deepen may create a row for a new request when the row is the durable planning route. Use `unrouted` only until evidence identifies an owner; keep volatile discussion in task context.
